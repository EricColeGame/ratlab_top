## What to Evaluate in Your First Session

### The role you enjoy

The official description presents three very different jobs. Try the role whose objective sounds interesting rather than forcing yourself through a single “correct” progression path:

- **Lab Rat:** escape and take revenge, or be tested on.
- **Scientist:** create mutations and test them on live subjects.
- **Security:** stop escapes or recapture runners.

### The session timer

The listing warns that your time resets every session. Observe how quickly tools become available in the role you chose, but do not treat a number from another player as an official unlock schedule. The developer has not published a table of times.

### The badge loop

Roblox lists six enabled badges for the experience, including **Test Subject** for reporting to the lab, **Out of the Lab** for clearing all three stages, and **For Science** for a first experiment. They are a useful way to check whether you have tried the main role loops. See the [badge guide](/en/guide/rat-lab-achievements) for the full list.
