The official Rat Lab listing gives the Lab Rat one clear goal: **attempt to escape and take revenge, or be tested on**. Roblox's public badge data adds two concrete objectives: clear all three stages and get outside, and use the bite to chew through a wall. That is enough for a reliable first route without pretending to know a map the developer has not published.

## A Reliable First Escape Attempt

### 1. Join as a Lab Rat

Open the [official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/105904727825849/Rat-Lab) and select the Lab Rat role. The official description frames the role around escape and revenge, so treat movement and escape opportunities as your first priority.

### 2. Learn the Containment Area

Before trying to force a route, identify the exits, the walls that react to the bite, and where security players are watching. The game's badge data confirms that biting a wall is a real escape action; it does not promise that every wall is breakable.

### 3. Use the Bite on a Breakable Wall

The **Through the Wall** badge is awarded for chewing through a wall with the bite. Use that as a cue to test obvious weak points, but do not assume a fixed wall location from a different game or a fan map.

### 4. Clear All Three Stages

The **Out of the Lab** badge states that the first full escape requires clearing all three stages and getting outside. Treat each stage as a checkpoint rather than expecting one shortcut to finish the entire run.

### 5. Reach the Outside

Getting outside is the condition named by the badge. Once you are out, the escape objective is complete even if you have not collected every optional badge.

## Playing Against Security

Security's official job is to stop rats from escaping and recapture runners. A useful rat route therefore has to account for more than the environment: watch patrols, avoid obvious choke points, and expect a recapture attempt after a failed escape. We do not have an official list of security tools or capture timers, so this guide does not invent them.
