The **Rat Lab trophy** is the clearest proof that you have fully mastered the lab — every puzzle solved, every hazard survived, every optional objective ticked off. It is also one of the easiest rewards to miss, because the **Rat Lab trophy** depends on side content that most players skip on their first run. If your completion tracker has been stuck at 90-something percent for weeks, this guide will show you exactly what is left to do.

Rat Lab rewards patience and observation more than raw speed. The trophy itself is not tied to a single boss fight or a lucky drop; it is a completion reward that checks a whole list of conditions at once. That means the fastest way to earn it is to understand the checklist first, then plan a single efficient run instead of replaying the same areas over and over.

## What the Rat Lab Trophy Actually Requires

Because Rat Lab is a compact, community-driven experience, exact unlock conditions can shift slightly between patches. The guidance below reflects what most players report and uses the broad structure that has stayed consistent: a main objective chain, a set of collectibles, and a handful of skill-based challenges.

In practice, the trophy is awarded when the game detects that all tracked requirements are complete at the same time. This is an important detail — several objectives are saved on a per-save basis rather than globally, so finishing one collectible on Save A and another on Save B may not count toward a single trophy award. Keep everything on one save file from start to finish.

| Requirement Category | What It Usually Covers | Tracked Where | Typical Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main objective chain | Reaching the final area and triggering the ending | Story progress menu | Low |
| Collectibles | Hidden notes, samples, or tokens scattered through each zone | Collection tab | Medium |
| Skill challenges | Timed escapes, stealth sections, no-hit rooms | Challenge log | High |
| Optional interactions | Talking to every NPC, activating every console | Dialogue/completion list | Low–Medium |
| Post-game cleanup | Returning to earlier areas with new abilities | Free-roam mode | Medium |

## Step-by-Step: How to Earn the Rat Lab Trophy

### Phase 1 — Before You Start

Spend five minutes setting up so you never have to repeat content. Pick one save slot, turn off any auto-cloud sync that might overwrite it, and note which zone you are entering. Player experience suggests that the majority of failed trophy attempts come down to messy save management rather than difficulty.

### Phase 2 — The Main Run

Push through the story without detouring. You want access to every tool and shortcut before you start hunting collectibles. Grab anything that is directly on your path, but do not chase secrets down dead ends yet — you will pass through these zones again with better gear.

### Phase 3 — The Cleanup Pass

Now move zone by zone and clear every remaining marker. Work backwards from the final area to the starting area, because that mirrors the order in which upgrades unlock new routes. A simple table keeps you honest:

| Cleanup Order | Zone Type | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Final area | Post-ending collectibles and unlocked doors |
| 2 | Mid-game zones | High ledges reachable with movement upgrades |
| 3 | Optional side rooms | Timed challenges and stealth sections |
| 4 | Starting area | Early locked doors that now open |

Once the checklist is clean, trigger the ending one more time. The Rat Lab trophy should register during the final sequence.

## Common Mistakes That Block the Rat Lab Trophy

These are the issues that show up again and again in community reports:

1. **Splitting progress across saves.** Anything tracked per save will not combine. This is the single most common reason a player finishes "everything" and still sees no trophy.
2. **Leaving a zone too early.** Some doors close behind you permanently. Sweep each area before you move on.
3. **Ignoring NPC dialogue.** Optional conversations often gate later interactions, and an unfinished conversation can silently block a requirement.
4. **Skipping timed challenges.** They are optional for the story but mandatory for the trophy.
5. **Quitting before the credits.** The final check usually runs at the end of the ending sequence, not at the final boss.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Trophy did not pop after ending | One requirement incomplete | Check the collection and challenge tabs row by row |
| Collectible counter stuck at 99% | A locked-door collectible was missed | Revisit zones with all upgrades equipped |
| Challenge log shows blank entries | Challenge never triggered | Re-enter the room and let the intro animation play |
| NPC still shows a dialogue icon | Conversation not exhausted | Talk repeatedly until lines repeat |

If you want the trophy in as few runs as possible, plan one full playthrough plus one cleanup pass. Two runs is realistic; one blind run plus a dedicated checklist run is the efficient pattern.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I earn the Rat Lab trophy on any difficulty?**
In most builds, yes. Difficulty settings generally affect combat and hazard damage, not the completion requirements themselves. Some players report that higher difficulties make the timed sections harder to clear, so drop the difficulty if you are struggling with a specific challenge.

**How long does it take to get the Rat Lab trophy?**
Player experience suggests two full runs is the realistic average: one to learn the layout and unlock everything, and one to clean up collectibles and challenges. Skipping the optional content on your first run actually saves time overall.

**What is the most commonly missed requirement?**
The timed challenge rooms. They are easy to walk past, they do not block story progress, and they are the last thing most players remember to do. Clear them as soon as you find them rather than saving them for a later session.

**Does the Rat Lab trophy unlock anything else?**
It typically sits at the top of the reward structure as a completion marker, confirming that every other tracked objective on your save has been finished. Think of it as the receipt for full completion rather than a key to new content.

## Final Thoughts

The Rat Lab trophy is less about skill and more about discipline. Stay on one save, sweep each zone before leaving, clear every timed challenge, and finish the story last. Do those four things and the trophy will register on schedule — no guesswork required.
