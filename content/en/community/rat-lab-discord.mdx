## How to Verify a Future Invite

1. Open the official game page or the verified Evil Science group.
2. Follow only a link published there.
3. Check that the server name and description identify Evil Science or Rat Lab.
4. Confirm the invite still resolves before sharing it.
5. Do not enter your Roblox password on a Discord verification page.

## Red Flags

- A page promises “working codes” in exchange for a Discord login.
- The invite leads to a generic Roblox fan server with no Rat Lab channel.
- A search advertisement uses a lookalike domain.
- A user asks for your Roblox cookie, password, or a “verification token”.
- A “moderator” offers mutations, badges, or tools for payment.
