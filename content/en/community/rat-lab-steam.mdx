## Practical Tips Before You Start Testing

Most of the friction players report on the **Rat Lab Steam** hub is fixable in under five minutes — it just isn't fixable *inside* the game. Here's the community-sourced shortlist.

| Reported Annoyance | Practical Workaround |
|---|---|
| No in-game key rebinding | Remap keys at the operating system or keyboard-software level |
| Uncapped frame rate heating the GPU | Cap FPS through your graphics driver's control panel |
| Achievements that quietly don't trigger | Follow a checklist guide and save before your final tests |
| A single puzzle blocking progress | Search the hub for the specific simulator name instead of the game's name |

Beyond settings, a few habits go a long way:

- **Read the AI's dialogue instead of skipping it.** The commentary often telegraphs mechanics, and the satire is the point of the game.
- **Treat each new puzzle type as a fresh tutorial.** Lab Rat deliberately bends genres, so habits from the last puzzle can actively hurt you in the next.
- **Screenshot your puzzle state.** It costs one second and saves you from re-deriving a setup after a failed attempt.
- **Don't grind a wall puzzle for an hour.** Search "Rat Lab" plus the puzzle's simulator name — someone has almost certainly asked about it.
- **Back up your save before the finale.** Achievement questers on the hub reported finishing every test without unlocking the "Great Job" achievement, so a pre-finale save is cheap insurance.

None of this is a knock on the game. It's the normal shape of a puzzle release with a passionate but small community: excellent content, thin official documentation, and a player base that figures out the rest together.

## FAQ

**Is "Rat Lab Steam" the same thing as Lab Rat?**
Yes. "Rat Lab" is a common word-order mix-up for *Lab Rat*, a satirical puzzle game on Steam. Searching either version lands you on the same store page and the same community hub, which is why so many threads there open with a variation of "sorry if this is the wrong name."

**Does Lab Rat have a full walkthrough?**
It does. A complete video walkthrough was recorded by a community member and shared on the hub with permission, and there's also a written achievement guide. Between the two, you can clear every puzzle and every achievement without guessing.

**Can you rebind keys in Lab Rat?**
Community reports say in-game key rebinding isn't available, which is one of the most common complaints on the hub. The standard workaround is remapping keys at the operating system or keyboard-software level before you launch the game.

**How do you unlock every achievement in Lab Rat?**
Follow a community checklist rather than playing blind. The "Great Job" achievement in particular was reported by at least one player who finished the game and every test without unlocking it, so tracking your progress and saving before the final stretch is the safest route to a full clear.
