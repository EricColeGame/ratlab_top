Rat Lab's achievements are Roblox badges, not Steam trading cards. Roblox's public badge API returned **six enabled badges** for universe 10598688389 on 2026-11-02. Their descriptions give reliable objectives even though the developer has not published a separate in-game achievement menu.

## All Six Official Badges

| Badge | Official requirement | Role or task |
|---|---|---|
| Test Subject | Report to the lab for the first time | First join |
| Out of the Lab | Clear all three stages and get outside for the first time | Lab Rat escape |
| Through the Wall | Chew your way through a wall with the bite | Lab Rat ability |
| For Science | Run your first experiment on a live subject | Scientist |
| New Specimen | Log a mutation nobody had recorded yet | Scientist discovery |
| Caught One | Recapture a runner and get them back into a closed cell | Security |

These descriptions are the authoritative part of this page. We have not added hidden badges, rarity tiers, XP values, or unlock times because the public badge data does not provide them.

## A Practical Way to Track Progress

Group the badges by the role you are playing:

- **First join:** Test Subject.
- **Lab Rat:** Out of the Lab and Through the Wall.
- **Scientist:** For Science and New Specimen.
- **Security:** Caught One.

This grouping prevents a common mistake: trying to earn every badge in one role. The official descriptions assign the experiment badges to a live-subject experiment and the recapture badge to a security action.

## How to Check Your Badges

Open the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/105904727825849/Rat-Lab) and use its badge section, or check your Roblox profile's badge list. A badge is tied to your Roblox account, so it remains visible after the session ends even though the in-game tool timer resets.

## Badge or Achievement?

For this experience, the two words refer to the same public system: Roblox badges. The page does not cover Steam achievements, Steam cards, PlayStation trophies, or cosmetic item trading. Those belong to other games or platforms and were the source of the earlier entity mix-up.
