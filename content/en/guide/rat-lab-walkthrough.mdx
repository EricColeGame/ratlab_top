This walkthrough is for the **Roblox experience Rat Lab by Evil Science**. It does not cover the Steam puzzle game *Lab Rat*, the Portal comic, or a malware-analysis challenge. The goal is to get you from the official game page into a useful first round without inventing a map or a stat table.

## Before You Join

Use the [official Rat Lab page](https://www.roblox.com/games/105904727825849/Rat-Lab). The place ID is 105904727825849 and the universe ID is 10598688389. The game is free, and the developer describes three roles: Lab Rat, Scientist, and Security.

The official listing also gives the key progression rule: tools unlock the longer you play, but the timer resets every session. Do not expect a permanent account level to carry your tool access forward.

## Step 1: Choose the Role You Want to Learn

| If you want to... | Choose | First objective |
|---|---|---|
| Escape and get outside | Lab Rat | Learn containment, find a breakable wall, clear three stages |
| Create and test mutations | Scientist | Run a first experiment on a live subject |
| Stop runners | Security | Recapture a runner and return them to a closed cell |

There is no official evidence that these are unlockable heroes. They are roles within the experience.

## Step 2: Play the Role Objective

### Lab Rat route

1. Learn the containment area and watch security movement.
2. Test the bite on a wall that reacts to it.
3. Progress through all three stages.
4. Reach the outside to satisfy the **Out of the Lab** badge.

The badge confirms the three-stage and bite objectives, but not a specific map layout. See the full [escape guide](/en/mechanics/rat-lab-escape).

### Scientist route

1. Join as a Scientist.
2. Create a mutation.
3. Test it on a live rat subject.
4. Record the result and check for **For Science**.
5. Keep experimenting toward **New Specimen**, which is awarded for logging a mutation nobody had recorded.

See [Mutations and Experiments](/en/mechanics/rat-lab-experiments) for the verified loop.

### Security route

1. Join as Security.
2. Watch for rats attempting to leave containment.
3. Recapture a runner and return them to a closed cell.
4. Check for **Caught One**.

## Step 3: Use Badges as Checkpoints

The six official badges are a better progress checklist than a made-up level table. The first-join badge is **Test Subject**. The role-specific badges are **Out of the Lab**, **Through the Wall**, **For Science**, **New Specimen**, and **Caught One**. The complete requirements are on the [badge guide](/en/guide/rat-lab-achievements).

## What to Ignore

- A hash, VirusTotal, or CyberDefenders walkthrough.
- A Portal 2 comic summary.
- A Steam store page for a different game called *Lab Rat*.
- A paid “tool unlock” or “mutation code” offer.
- Exact keybinds, mutation percentages, or map coordinates without a developer source.
